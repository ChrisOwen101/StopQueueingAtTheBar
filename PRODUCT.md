# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS in `public/`, no build step, served by Cloudflare Workers assets. A small Worker (`src/worker.js`) handles `/api/sign` and `/api/count`, backed by Neon Postgres through Hyperdrive. Confirmed by the user as a binding constraint for new work.

## Users
- **Primary:** pub-goers and the general UK public who stand in single-file queues at the bar. They arrive via shares or word of mouth, want a laugh, and may sign the petition and start "clustering".
- **Secondary:** landlords and bar staff at UK pubs, bars and breweries, who can adopt The Cluster and later use the beer mats.
- **Tertiary:** press and trade bodies (CAMRA, BBPA, UKHospitality, London Brewers' Alliance).

## Product Purpose
A comedy campaign against single-file queueing at the bar. Since COVID (2020 distancing stickers), people line up in one file instead of spreading along the bar, which wastes room, blocks seating and leaves most of the bar and staff idle. The goal is to get people to stop, in a funny way. It is as much comedy as campaign, so not everything is serious. Success is petition signatures plus pubs adopting The Cluster (beer mats now planned, pub pledges later).

## Positioning
Mock-scientific, dry British humour (format borrowed from cuberule.com): "the Bar Rule", rulings, an alignment chart and animated pub simulations, anchored by one real data point. Neighbouring campaigns (e.g. @QueuesPub, running since 2023) do not use this format.

## Operating Context
UK pubs, bars and breweries. The wrong behaviour is **The Single File**; the right one is **The Cluster**. Pre-COVID, people spread along the bar and the barperson or landlord knew who was next. The page is a long single scroll of full-screen colour blocks, with a fixed timeline rail tracking 8 chapters (`#covid`, `#after`, `#hackney`, `#rule`, `#howto`, `#rulings`, `#mats`, `#petition-section`). Social meta and icons are generated from `public/logo-side.svg` (`npm run build:icons`); beer mat artwork is generated by `npm run build:mats`.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Petition form (`POST /api/sign`) and running count (`GET /api/count`, cached 60s); duplicate emails silently ignored.
- Animated single-file vs cluster pub simulations; count-ups; scroll-reveal; timeline rail. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected and the page works without JS.
- Beer mats are "coming soon"; nothing is for sale and there is no ordering route yet.
- **Gender-neutral language, always:** barperson, bar staff, landlord; they/them/their for staff. Never barman, barmaid, he/she/him/his/her.
- No photos. Hard-coded domains are avoided (the Worker injects the origin into `data-abs` tags).
- Legal and trust: UK GDPR and PECR (consent for campaign email, opt-out). Privacy notice and signature deletion route are not yet built. Spam protection (Turnstile or WAF rate limit) is not yet in place.
- Not affiliated with Hackney Church Brew Co, and the footer says so.
- Undecided: custom domain (current beer mat address is `https://dontqueueatthebar.co.uk/`), live URL, merch/shop route.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: "Don't Queue At The Bar" (earlier "Stop Queueing at the Bar", "Bar the Line / The Bar Party").
- Voice: direct, dry British humour; short, punchy lines; one idea per section.
- Terminology: The Single File, The Cluster, the Bar Rule, Additional Bar Rulings (The Round, The Hoverer, The Phantom Queue, The Landlord).
- Look commitments recorded from the existing site only: very bold, huge lowercase type, one idea per full-screen colour block, simple animated diagrams, no photos. Assets: `public/logo-side.svg` and the generated icon, share image and beer mat files.

## Evidence on Hand
Source: East London Times, 29 Aug 2026 (reporting Broadsheet London, MyLondon, YouGov).
- Hackney Church Brew Co, 14 Aug 2026 post "A BAR IS NOT A POST OFFICE"; bar about 20 ft with 4 staff.
- Like-for-like sales up **25%** the following Thursday (£3.50 pints), steady footfall, higher spend per head. This is the brewery's own claim.
- YouGov: **40%** prefer single-file, **39%** prefer along the bar; London only 37% single-file; Midlands, North and Wales 42%; ages 25 to 49 favour along the bar most.
- Everything else (sim scores, alignment chart, rulings) is jokes and must stay marked as such.
- Absent: testimonials, bar staff quotes, pub pledges, press coverage of this campaign, sales figures. Do not fabricate any.
- Signature export lives in `exports/` (personal data; never surface it in the UI).

## Product Principles
1. Funny first, but never at the cost of the real argument: one real, cited fact sits underneath the jokes.
2. Keep real and joke figures distinct; never invent evidence.
3. One idea per block: the page should be understood by someone scrolling quickly on a phone in a pub.
4. Signing the petition must stay low-friction and privacy-respecting.
5. Stay gender-neutral and kind: tease behaviour, never shame people or pubs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No formal standard recorded. Existing commitments: `prefers-reduced-motion`, works without JS. Colour-block contrast, focus states, `aria-live` for sim totals and keyboard use of the timeline rail are open items (see IDEAS.md). Gender-neutral copy is a firm inclusion rule.
