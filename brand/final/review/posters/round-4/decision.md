# Posters (notice family), round 4: the decision

Read with `designer.md` and `marketing.md` in this folder. The two reviews agreed again, so this is a short merge.

## The user's two asks

- **"Please don’t single-file queue. Cheers."** replaces "Please don’t queue. Cheers." on every sheet that has the line (the two instruction posters and the alternate). It breaks after "queue.", never inside the phrase, because the whole line is wider than the foot's column in every font; the roundel on the instruction poster gives up 6 mm to pay for the second line.
- **"Queue along the bar, not away from it."** earns two places and changes a third:
  - **A new instruction poster**, `poster-along`: ink, "ALONG / THE BAR. / NOT AWAY / FROM IT." at 30 mm with ALONG and AWAY in amber, the roundel at 84 mm, the new polite line, "Sign the petition."
  - **A new notice**, `notice-along`: "QUEUE / ALONG / THE BAR." over the roundel and the prohibition column side by side, "✓ ALONG" (amber) and "AWAY" as labels, the house line, the ask, the foot.
  - **The notice's ask** becomes "We kindly ask that you queue / along the bar, not away from it. / The bar staff know who’s next.", the stressed words at 900 inside the 600 line, and the sign drops to 100 mm.

## Rules the round adds

- **Stress.** A stressed word is set in Amber Pint, so the sheet is on ink; every word keeps 900. Where amber isn't available (mono, or a line of running text) the stressed word is 900 and the rest of its line keeps its own weight. The family never underlines: its stacked line-height has no room for a rule. Only the line that holds a stressed word is affected, never the rest of the sheet.
- **One amber meaning per sheet.** On the along poster amber is the stress; on the two-pictogram notice it is the tick and ALONG ("this one"), and nothing else on that sheet is amber, including its headline.
- **Uppercase survives** a sentence-length headline: the amber words carry it. The lowercase render is the record, not a sheet.
- **The phrasing on the sheets is "not away from it"**; the user's spoken form, "not away from the bar", is the source and lives in the brief.

## Dropped

Weight as the colour device (mono fallback only), the underline, the fragment "ALONG. / NOT AWAY." (an alternate headline, recorded, not shipped), the lowercase version. The Round 4 render of the notice with the ask has a layout bug (the stress device leaked onto the headline); it stays here as the record and is fixed in Round 5.

## Round 5

The final set becomes five sheets: `notice-ink`, `notice-along`, `poster-green`, `poster-along`, `poster-gap`, each in colour and mono at A4 and A5, with the Arial check and the print check, and the brief updated.
