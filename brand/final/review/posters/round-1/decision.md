## Rules the round settled

- **The mount:** the field is inset 8 mm at A4 (5.6 mm at A5), a drawn white frame, so a home printer's unprintable edge falls inside it and the frame prints even. Content stays 12 mm inside the field. The mono versions draw a 1 mm black line on the same edge.
- **Paper white, not cream, on a field.** The fields are the palette hexes; the marks and type on them are white (the paper), black on the monos.
- **The new pictogram:** the prohibition sign is a ring and slash (the roundel's ring and stroke, ISO 3864's slash) over four `FIG` glyphs in a vertical column with gaps. The grammar: the Cluster is a horizontal row, the Single File a vertical column. It always has its slash, never a bar, and the figures never touch, so it can't be read as the roundel.
- **One roundel per sheet.** On the notice it is the mark at the foot; on the instruction poster it is the pictogram, and the mark, at once. The stamp is on neither; the name is in the headline.
- **Uppercase headlines** on the notice family (`.mega.upper`'s licence). Sentence case for everything else.
- **Ink:** every colour sheet needs its mono for the office printer, and the download page says which is which. The colour sheets are for a colour printer, a print shop or a laminator.
- **One scan reason per sheet**, the code plain and level H, the address the bare domain at 7.6 mm (the A5 floor).
