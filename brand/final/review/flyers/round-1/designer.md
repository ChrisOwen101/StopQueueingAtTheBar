# Flyers, round 1: designer's notes

Reviewed 13 Oct 2026 against `brand/BRIEF.md` and the "Brand marks", "Typography" and "Beer mats" sections of `DESIGN.md`. Sizes below are measured from the 150 dpi A4 renders (1 mm is about 5.9 px, so take them as plus or minus 0.3 mm). A5 figures are the A4 figure times 0.707. The renders are set in Inter, so I have judged layout, scale, spacing, the marks and the ink, not the letterforms; Avenir Next Heavy sets a little wider than Inter Black, so allow about 5% on every headline measure before trusting a line break.

## What all six share

- **Margins:** every sheet keeps the 10 mm safe margin. The only thing not on it is C1-A's QR code, which sits 13 mm from the right edge while everything else sits on 10 mm.
- **Ink:** C1-A 12.8%, C1-B 9.8%, C2-A 10.2%, C2-B 11.2%, C3-A 16.5%, C3-B 13.6%. All ink-light. The only large solid field is C3's bar (see C3).
- **The footer is the same bolt-on on all six:** "Sign the petition." at 3.2 to 3.9 mm x-height (2.3 to 2.8 mm at A5) over the address at 2.2 mm x-height (1.6 mm at A5), 500 weight. The address fails the 2.5 mm test on A5 on every sheet, and it is the one line on the page that has to be read exactly. Fix once, use on all (see "The web address").
- **QR codes:** 28.5 mm on the B faces (0.68 mm modules; 20 mm and 0.48 mm modules at A5) and 32 to 33 mm on the A faces (0.77 to 0.8 mm modules; 23 mm and 0.55 mm at A5). A5 is on the edge of what a phone reads from a photocopy. The code is 41 modules a side because the address is 56 characters with the scheme; a short domain would drop a version or two and give bigger modules at the same size.
- **Copy:** typographic apostrophes throughout (don’t, It’s, who’s). Gender-neutral throughout (barperson, bar staff, they). "Twenty orders, 75 seconds each. One spot on the bar, or four staff along it." is verbatim on all three B faces and all three carry the "worked example" tag. Good.
- **Kickers** ("how to do the cluster", "house rules") are lowercase, medium weight and wide-tracked. That isn't our Label style (900, uppercase, 0.08 em), and DESIGN.md gives uppercase to labels only. At 2.7 mm x-height (1.9 mm at A5) they also fail the A5 test. Reset them or drop them.
- **The "worked example" tag** is the site's Tag (pill, thick border, tilted -3 degrees), which is right, but its text is 2.7 mm x-height (1.9 mm at A5). The label that keeps the claim honest is the least legible thing next to the claim. It needs to be at least 3.5 mm x-height on A4.

## Concept 1, "Stamp first"

**Face A.** The stamp is 147 mm across, centred, top edge 26 mm down. It leads, as the brief asks, and it is the mat front at wall size, so the mat, the flyer and the site all say the same thing in the same shape. At a tenth it is the one of the three where the brand reads first: "don't queue" is about 24 mm x-height, good to ten metres; "at the bar." about 12 mm; the tagline (5.9 mm x-height, cap height about 8.5 mm) is readable at three metres but only just, which is the floor for a poster line. One idea per sheet: yes.

Faults, all spacing, none of concept:

- **Clear space is broken.** A 147 mm stamp needs 18.4 mm clear on every side. The tagline's x-height starts about 13.5 mm under the ring, so it sits about 5 mm inside the stamp's clear space. The brief says nothing enters it.
- **Dead band.** About 40 mm of nothing between the tagline and the footer (205 to 245 mm down). The stamp and line are composed; the footer is parked.
- **The roundel is on the sheet twice,** 20 mm apart in the same band: once at bottom left (18.5 mm ring, clear space clean) and once inside the QR. The flyer doesn't need two.
- **The roundel in the QR helps on the mat and hurts here.** The tile is 14 mm (42% of the side, 17% of the area, as on the mat), the roundel 12 mm wide, the ring stroke 0.9 mm and each figure's head 1.5 mm. Its own rule asks for 1.5 mm clear and it gets 0.8 mm to the nearest module. At A5 the stroke is 0.64 mm and the heads 1.06 mm on 0.55 mm modules. A controlled mat print copes; a mono laser with toner gain, then a photocopy, eats both the roundel and the error correction it costs. On flyers the QR should be plain.

**Face B.** The best of the three B faces. The numeral column (11 mm cap height, flush left, 22 mm gutter to the text) sets a strong rhythm, the four steps fall at even intervals, the rule separates the steps from the arithmetic, and the tag sits on the headline's line at the right, which is where the site puts it. Step heads at 5.9 mm x-height hold at A5 (4.2 mm). "25 minutes. or six." at 8.1 mm x-height holds (5.7 mm).

Faults:

- Step bodies 2.9 mm x-height (2.0 mm at A5): fail. The worked-example paragraph 2.9 mm: fail. Both need to be at least 3.6 mm on A4.
- The stamp at bottom right is 25.1 mm, which is 17.7 mm at A5, just under the 18 mm minimum. It has to be at least 25.5 mm on A4, so make it 30 mm and stop worrying.
- 47 mm dead band between the paragraph and the footer.
- Kicker and tag as above.

## Concept 2, "House rule"

**Face A.** As a poster it is the best read of the three: "this bar does the cluster." at 16 mm x-height per line reads from six metres, and at a tenth it is the only sheet where the words win outright. It is also the right voice for a thing a pub prints: a landlord putting it up is saying "this is how we do it here", which is the brief's purpose, and the sub explains the term in one breath. "The bar staff know who's next. They always did." is a good, neutral line.

Faults:

- **The stamp doesn't lead.** It is a 43 mm letterhead (30 mm at A5) in the corner and the headline is the mark. The brief wrote one rule for flyers and this concept breaks it. At a tenth the stamp is a dot with "don't queue" just legible.
- **To a stranger at three metres, "the cluster" is jargon** until they come close enough for the 5.4 mm sub, which reads at about two and a half metres.
- **76 mm of empty page** between the sub and the footer (175 to 250 mm down), a third of the sheet, and not a composed third: the footer floats.
- "house rules" kicker is orphaned top right and off-system (see above). "always did." is a one-word last line.
- Roundel bottom left 16.6 mm ring, clear space clean. Fine.

**Face B.** The ruled list is a nice literal pun on "rules", and the 1.2 mm rules would help a muddy photocopy hold its structure. The tag inside the box, aligned to the headline, is the best-attached tag of the three.

Faults:

- **Bad headline break:** "the rules of this / bar." leaves "bar." alone on line two. It must be "the rules / of this bar."
- **Too much linework.** Five rules plus a boxed panel plus the tag's own border. The box is within the system (thick outline, chunky corners) but it turns the worked example into a coupon. Keep the rules or the box, not both.
- Step bodies 2.9 mm, paragraph 2.7 mm, tag 2.7 mm: all fail at A5. Numerals with full stops ("1.") are smaller and weaker than C1's bare figures.
- Stamp bottom right 29.5 mm (20.9 mm at A5): fine. 27 mm dead band: the least of the three.

## Concept 3, "The bar"

**Face A.** The idea is good and the bar is the strongest shape on any of the six at a tenth. But three things are shouting at once: the stamp (70 mm, with its own words), "it's a pub. not a post office." beside it at 12.5 mm x-height, and "spread out." under the bar at 18.6 mm, which is the biggest thing on the page. At a tenth the order of reading is "spread out.", then the stamp, then the tagline: the opposite of the brief's hierarchy, and the eye bounces between the three.

Specific faults:

- **"spread out." isn't spread.** It is set at the house's tight tracking, so the words don't do the thing the concept note says they do. The gag only exists if the letters are visibly spaced along the bar like people, and that breaks the tight-tracking rule for the sake of one joke. Either commit to the joke as the single exception or lose it; as rendered it is neither.
- **The headline breaks "not a post / office."** The joke lands on "post office" as one unit; split, it reads wrong at a glance.
- **The bar is a redraw.** 12.9 mm by 190 mm of solid black, margin to margin, 2,450 mm² and the only large solid field in the round. The ink total is still fine (16.5%), but a full-width solid stripe is where cheap lasers band and inkjets cockle, and it is the mark's bar without the mark's ring, which is the exact line the brief draws under "don't redraw it". As a divider it is strong; as brand it is a loose part.
- Sub 4.6 mm x-height, reads at two metres; "did." on its own line. 56 mm dead band.
- Stamp 70.5 mm top left, clear space 8.8 mm, clean.

**Face B.** Four columns are right for a landscape bar runner and wrong for a portrait sheet that will be printed at A5.

- Column bodies are 2.2 mm x-height (1.6 mm at A5) on a 42 mm measure: the smallest text on any sheet, in the narrowest column. At A5 each column is 30 mm wide.
- Column heads break mid-phrase: "walk to the / bar.", "catch an / eye.", "trust the / barperson." Column 3 runs five lines, column 2 runs three; the bottoms are ragged.
- The numerals are 19 mm tall, the largest thing on the page after the headline, and they are decoration.
- "four easy / steps." and "25 minutes. / or six." both break over two lines for no gain, and the tag floats top right, unattached to the figure it qualifies.
- Stamp 25.4 mm (18.0 mm at A5): on the limit. Second 10 mm bar: same objections.

## The web address

`dont-queue-at-the-bar.aged-mouse-6395.workers.dev` is 48 characters with four hyphens and a random animal in the middle. Nobody will type it, and printed on a wall it tells the room the campaign is temporary. Two things:

1. **Decide the domain before anything is printed.** `scripts/build-mats.mjs` already encodes `https://dontqueueatthebar.co.uk/` on the mat back, so right now the mat and the flyer point at different addresses. The flyer should carry whatever the mat carries. (The domain is still an open item in AGENTS.md; this is the moment to close it.)
2. **Until then, set it so it survives A5:** 600 weight, x-height 3.6 mm on A4 (2.5 mm at A5), two lines broken at the first dot with the dot ending line one, `dont-queue-at-the-bar.` / `aged-mouse-6395.workers.dev`. At that size it needs about 80 mm of measure, which fits between the roundel and the QR. "Sign the petition." sits above it at 4.5 mm x-height, 900 weight. The QR is the real route; the address is the fallback and should look like one.

## Decision

**Take Concept 1 as the skeleton for both faces, fold Concept 2's house voice into Face B, and drop Concept 3.**

Why C1: it is the only one where the stamp leads, which is the one flyer rule in the brief; it passes the poster test at a tenth with the brand reading first; it is the lightest on ink; and every fault is a size or a gap, none is the idea. Why fold in C2: a pub prints this, and "house rules" and "the rules of this bar." are the voice of a pub telling its customers how things work here, which is what Face B is for in the hand. C2-A's headline is the better three-metre read, but it can only lead by demoting the stamp, so it can't be Face A. I would render it once more as an alternative line under the stamp (see below) and pick in Round 2. Why not C3: three voices on one sheet, the bar is a redraw of the mark's bar, the spread-out joke isn't in the render, it has the most ink and the worst B face at A5.

## Round 2: concrete changes (A4 mm; A5 is 0.707 of each)

**Face A (C1-A, repaired)**

1. Stamp 140 mm across, centred, top edge 28 mm down. Clear space 17.5 mm all round; nothing in it.
2. The line under it: one line, sentence case, 600 weight, x-height 7 mm (cap height about 10 mm), centred, measure no more than 170 mm, first line's x-height no higher than 17.5 mm below the ring. Render two: "It’s a pub. Not a post office." (brand, matches the OG image and the hero) and "This bar does the cluster. Spread out, they know who’s next." (house). Pick one in Round 2; the layout is the same.
3. Close the dead band: anchor the footer on the bottom margin and centre the stamp-and-line group in the space above it, so the gap above the stamp and the gap below the line are equal.
4. Footer, 36 mm tall, on the 10 mm margins: roundel 20 mm at left (18.5 mm ring, 2.5 mm clear); "Sign the petition." and the two-line address as above, left-aligned beside it; plain QR 36 mm at right including its quiet zone, flush to the right margin, error correction M, no roundel inside. One roundel per sheet.

**Face B (C1-B skeleton, C2 voice)**

1. Kicker "HOUSE RULES" in the Label style: 900, uppercase, 0.08 em, cap height 5 mm. Or no kicker.
2. Headline "the rules of this bar." on two lines, "the rules / of this bar.", x-height 12 mm per line, as C1-B's headline.
3. Steps as C1-B: bare numerals at 11 mm cap height, 22 mm gutter, heads at 6 mm x-height, bodies at 3.6 mm x-height, 600 weight, line height 1.3, measure no more than 150 mm. No rules between steps (keep the page open), one 2 mm rule above the worked example as C1-B has.
4. Worked example: "25 minutes. or six." one line at 8 mm x-height. The tag at 3.5 mm x-height in a pill about 11 mm tall, 0.6 mm border, -3 degrees, sat on the rule and overlapping the headline's cap line at the right, as the site does. Paragraph at 3.6 mm x-height, 600 weight, two lines, measure no more than 110 mm. No box.
5. Vertical rhythm: headline block, steps, worked example, footer, with equal gaps. If space is left over it goes into the bodies, not into one hole at the bottom.
6. Footer: QR 36 mm at left (plain, level M), "Sign the petition." and the address in the middle, stamp 30 mm at right (21 mm at A5, above the 18 mm minimum), 3.75 mm clear.

**Both**

7. Tag, kicker and the address as above; nothing on either sheet under 3.5 mm x-height on A4.
8. Every element on the 10 mm margins, including the QR.
9. Generate the flyers from `scripts/lib/brand.mjs` through `brand/final/build.mjs` like the other marks, so the stamp and roundel are never redrawn (`brand/final/flyer-a5.svg` is an older layout with both marks at the top; Round 2 should supersede it). I see `pdf-lib` has been added to `package.json`; fine for writing the PDF, as long as the marks still come from the module.
10. Render A5 at 0.707 and check it, then print one A4 and one A5 of each face on a mono laser and run both through a photocopier, and scan the QR from the copy. That is the real test, not the PNG.
