run a pub?

Four staff.
Four pouring.

48 an hour.
Or 192.

Orders through one spot on the bar, or four staff along it.

worked example

On a Friday at 90 orders an hour, one spot on the bar serves 48. The other 42 stand in a line holding about £224 of pints. Four staff along the bar pour the lot.

Arithmetic, not an audit: 75 seconds an order, a pint an order, a line that ends at one spot on the bar, the average pint (£5.34, Morning Advertiser, Apr 2026). The Friday is made up. Your pub is yours.
