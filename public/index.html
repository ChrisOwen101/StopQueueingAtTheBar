the problem

The Single File

Everyone queues in one line. One barperson works.
The other three watch. It’s slow, and it gets in the way.

✗ not recommended

the solution

The Cluster

Spread out along the bar.
Everyone gets a patch of bar, and everyone gets served quicker.

worked example

Simple arithmetic, not a survey: 75 seconds an order, a single file that ends at one spot on the bar (they do), and four staff for the cluster. Real pubs are messier. Illustrative, like the sims.

a short history

Blame 2020.
Mostly.

Eight stops. Quicker than the queue.

  1. before2020

    The invisible queue.

    “The bar counter is the only place in Britain in which anything is sold or served without the formation of a queue.”

    Kate Fox, Passport to the Pub, 1996

  2. 20 Mar2020

    Pubs shut.

    The Prime Minister asks pubs to close ‘tonight as soon as they reasonably can’. Ordering at the bar won’t be rule-free again for 486 days.

    GOV.UK, PM statement, 20 Mar 2020

  3. 4 Jul2020

    Pubs reopen. Carefully.

    Table service where possible. Stickers on the floor. Don’t gather at the bar. The rules never said ‘queue at the bar’. Somewhere in here, people started to.

    GOV.UK guidance for pubs, 2020 (later revision)

  4. 24 Sep2020

    Table service. By law.

    Sit down. Order from your seat. Home by ten. For 298 days, the bar is something you look at. Lockdowns permitting.

    SI 2020/1029 (England), 24 Sep 2020

  5. 19 Jul2021

    The bar is back.

    The rules go at 11.55pm the night before. You can walk up to the bar again. Legally. Nobody tells the queue.

    SI 2021/848 (England), 19 Jul 2021

  6. July2023

    Someone notices.

    Time Out asks: ‘Why have people started queuing single-file in pubs?’ Two years after the rules ended.

    Time Out, July 2023

  7. 18 Sep2025

    Britain splits.

    YouGov asks which you’d prefer. Single file, 40%. Along the bar, 39%. Don’t know, 21%. One point in it.

    YouGov, 18 Sep 2025, 5,914 GB adults

  8. today

    over five years since you were allowed back at the bar.

    Still queueing. That’s long enough.

    Counted from 19 Jul 2021, England (SI 2021/848)

1 of 8

The rules here are England’s. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland set their own; Wales was the last to drop table service, on 28 January 2022.
