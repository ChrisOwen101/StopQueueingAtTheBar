introducing

The Bar
Rule

the grand unified theory
of ordering a drink

① The Single File

Wait at the back. One barman works.
The other three watch.

✗ not recommended

② The Cluster

Everyone gets a patch of bar.
Everyone gets served. Nobody builds a snake.

✓ the correct answer

Same pub.
Same Thursday.

Same four staff. Same pints.
Keep an eye on the scores.

in four easy steps

How to do
The Cluster

① Walk to
the bar.

Not behind someone. To the bar.
It's the long wooden thing.

② Pick
a gap.

Any gap. Leave some elbow room.
This is not a cuddle.

③ Catch
an eye.

A nod. A raised eyebrow.
A tenner held with quiet confidence.
No waving. No clicking.

④ Trust
the barman.

He knows who's next.
He always knows.
It's his entire job.

The Phantom Queue

A queue with no bar at the end of it.
Past the toilets. Round the pool table.
Nobody knows where it stops.
Nobody's brave enough to ask.
