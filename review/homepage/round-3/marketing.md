## 3. Would anything bias the poll?

- The two buttons are equal in size, border, weight and colour on the pill and on the screen; neither is amber; Stop is first on both (a primacy effect exists either way and was declared in Round 2); the hover fill is the same cream for both (`proto.css:196`).
- The pill asks "Stop the queue?" and the screen "Stop the single file?": two wordings, one stored value, as decided; the poll's own caveat ("self-selecting and unscientific") stays on the results (`proto.js:162-163`).
- The sims' live scores sit a few hundred pixels from the pill: that is the campaign's argument and the poll is self-selecting by design; nothing in the pill itself leans.
- The one lean found is item 3: the phone pill opens with focus on Stop. Fix it.

## 4. No JS and reduced motion, as read in the code

**No JS.** No fixed element at all (`proto.css:131`); the poll screen's form and the petition form post to the Worker (`home.html:157`, 201) and the Worker answers with its HTML pages; the hero shows neither a count nor another line; the how-to ships its finished frame; the trailer says "over five years"; `/history` reads top to bottom with every source link in the markup; the JSON-LD is a data block, not a script, so the CSP doesn't block it (confirm in the console after deploy). The only no-JS defect is the Worker's words (item 5), which the PR fixes.

**Reduced motion.** The pill swaps states with no transition; the sims hold a pre-rolled still; the count-up is skipped on the home trailer and both counts on `/history`; the bars show at full value; the home trailer has no stickers. The one gap is `/history`'s sticker slap (item 14).
