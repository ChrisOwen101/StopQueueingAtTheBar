## The five open questions, settled

1. **Order:** hero → `#verdict` → `#poll` → `#petition-section` → `#howto` → `#test` → `#history` (trailer) → `#pubs` → footer. Marketing's Round 2 order. The vote is the cheap ask and the natural reply to the proof; the poll's results screen already says "sign", so the form is the next screen rather than an anchor jump over the pinned how-to; the form's "Now go and stand at the bar" leads into the screen that shows how. Two ask screens in a row is not two asks at once: the pill is hidden on both. The designer's petition-at-screen-3 with the poll after the test is not built; it is a one-section move the team can try later. Rail, in order: `#verdict`, `#poll`, `#petition-section`, `#howto`, `#test`, `#history`, `#pubs`.
2. **Stop / Keep** replaces For / Against everywhere in the prototypes: the pill, the poll screen's buttons ("Stop it · spread along the bar" / "Keep it · stay in line"), the chart rows, the quips. Two of three chose it, and the copywriter's withdrawal rested on a misreading (they thought both others had kept For / Against). The copywriter's condition stands too: the pill never drops its question. In the prototype the poll words live in `proto.js`; at merge they fold into `script.js` and `pollWords` in `src/worker.js` together.
3. **Money:** the tile's big number is orders, "48 an hour. Or 192." (3,600 ÷ 75; × 4), with the one money sentence under it: on the worked example's Friday, 42 orders an hour stand in a line holding about £224 of pints (42 × £5.34), and the exact honesty line from `round-2/marketing.md` §4. Not £769. The marketing specialist adds the three register rows (the pint price promoted from Reserves with its URL, the derived £224, the derived 192) and the avoid-list lines during Round 3.
4. **Hero:** the "Run a pub? →" door, a 40px outline pill top right level with the lockup (36px on phones), linking to `#pubs`. Navigation, not an ask. The signature count line is built but shows only from 500 signatures via `/api/count` (marketing's threshold), as a `.small` fact with a link in the copywriter's words ("12,345 have signed. Add your name ↓"); below 500 and without JS the hero carries no count and no other line. No vote line in the hero.
5. **The pill:** desktop shows the kicker, the question and both buttons at rest, so a vote is one tap; the pill's only amber is its kicker, never a filled button (the design system's One Amber Rule; the rail already puts that amber on every screen); phones get a collapsed ink "Vote" pill bottom right that expands in place (two taps, zero viewport cost), not a bottom bar. After a vote: the tally with "you" on the voter's side and one next step by side (Stop → "Sign the petition" to `#petition-section`; Keep → "Take the test" to `/hazard`). A returning voter loads straight into the voted state. After signing the pill goes. **Without JavaScript there is no fixed element at all**: the poll screen is at 2 vp and its form posts to the Worker as now, which is simpler than a no-JS pill and has nothing to get wrong.
